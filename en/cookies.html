Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 25, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies and similar technologies (such as browser local storage) are small files stored on your device so that the site and the applications can work.

2. Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary: authentication, session persistence, security and basic preferences (for example, language or light/dark theme). Without them the Service does not work.
  • Functional: they remember settings to improve your experience.

Appogio applications focus on operational data; we do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies for commercial purposes.

3. How to manage them

You can delete or block cookies from your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent you from signing in or using the Service.
