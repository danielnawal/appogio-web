Reseller network

And you can too

You don't stop at being a distributor: we create white-label sub-distributors that hang from you. Each with its own brand, its own domain and its own customers, but all inside your platform and under your terms.

Sell more without hiring more

Each reseller goes out to sell on their own, with their own brand and their own people. You grow into cities and sectors where you have no team, without adding a single salary.

You set each one’s price

You set the reseller’s price and they set the end customer’s. The difference is your margin, and you control it reseller by reseller.

Each with their own brand and domain

The reseller has their own name, logo, colours and web address. Their end customer sees neither the reseller’s brand above nor yours below.

The customer base stays yours

The reseller hangs off you: you see their units, you invoice them, and you can activate or suspend them. If a reseller leaves, their customers do not leave with the platform.

It is the same model you have with us, one floor down: you are the invisible engine of your reseller, just as we are for you.
