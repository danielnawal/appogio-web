Features

Full control of your public transit

Real-time bus tracking

Location, speed and status of every bus live on the map, so you know instantly where each unit on the route is.

Schedule and headway control

Control schedule compliance and the headway between buses, preventing bunching or gaps in the frequency.

Geofences and defined route paths

Define each route’s path with geofences; detect deviations and measure the bus’s progress along its planned route.

Real-time control center

The whole operation on one screen: buses, routes, schedules and incidents, to make decisions in the moment.

Incident logging

Log operational events and incidents with their time and location, for audit and continuous service improvement.

Analytics and GTFS-RT compatibility

Service indicators and reports, with GTFS-RT feed compatibility to inform passengers and external systems.
