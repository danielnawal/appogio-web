What it does

Everything WhatStar solves

Link the number with a QR code

You scan it once from the phone, just like WhatsApp Web, and the number stays connected to the platform.

Several numbers at once

Each client or department can have its own: sales, support, fleet alerts.

Automatic fleet alerts

Tracking software events go out by WhatsApp with the wording you define: speeding, geofence entry and exit, power cut, and whatever else you configure.

Bulk sending, under control

Campaigns and notices to contact lists, with the sending pace controlled so the number is not exposed.

Automatic replies

A chatbot handles the repetitive part and leaves to a human what genuinely needs one.

Everything is on record

Conversations and sends are stored on the platform, not on the phone of an employee who leaves tomorrow.
