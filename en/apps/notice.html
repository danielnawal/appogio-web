Features

Operational communication that really works

Integrated WhatsApp

Send alerts, reports and messages directly to the driver's WhatsApp. No additional apps: the driver receives it on the phone they already use.

Push notifications

Push notifications segmented by driver, vehicle, route or zone. With delivery receipt and read confirmation from the PilotOS app.

Automatic GPS alerts

Triggers automatic messages for geofence events, speeding, unscheduled stops or off-route vehicles — no per-event configuration needed.

Email and SMS

Backup channel for critical alerts. Configure escalation rules: if the driver doesn't respond in X minutes, escalate to the supervisor by email.

Message templates

Create reusable templates with dynamic variables ({name}, {vehicle}, {speed}) to speed up sending recurring operational messages.

History and audit

Complete record of all communications: who sent, to whom, when and whether it was delivered. Exportable for audits and compliance reports.

Multi-channel

One message, all channels

Define an alert rule once and Notice automatically decides the best channel based on event type, urgency and recipient preferences.

Critical alerts → WhatsApp Higher open rate than any other channel
Operational notifications → Push Instant delivery to the driver's device
Reports and summaries → Email Automatic daily/weekly reports for managers
