Service continuity

Know which devices stopped reporting, and why

A device that is off means a lost invoice and a client walking away. FleetWatch checks your fleet every five minutes, tells you how long each device has been down and gives you the likely cause, with its confidence level.

Request a demo
What it does

Everything FleetWatch solves

The whole picture at a glance

How many devices are reporting, how many are down and how many never reported since they were registered. The number at the top already tells you if the day is going well or badly.

How long it has been down

The time breakdown separates the urgent from the old: under an hour, 1 to 6, 6 to 24, 1 to 7 days, 7 to 30, and over 30 days.

The likely cause, without guessing

Out of service, a customer-wide failure, no data or SIM, vehicle not in use... and when there is not enough information it says so plainly instead of making up a cause.

The ones that fail again and again

The list of repeat offenders: the ones that go down, come back and go down again. That is where you lose the customer without noticing.

By end customer and by fleet

The ranking shows which customers have the most devices down, so you can start with the one closest to complaining.

WhatsApp alerts

No need to log in and check: the alert arrives on its own when something goes down, with the device and the affected customer.
