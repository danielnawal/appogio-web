Features

One phone, one more tracking unit

Turns the phone into a GPS unit

The person shows up on your tracking platform as one more unit, next to your vehicles, with no extra equipment.

Login with tracker IMEI or ID

The operator creates the unit on the platform and hands over the credentials; the person logs into TrackPhone with that IMEI or ID and starts reporting.

Explicit consent, only once

On first login the person agrees to be tracked. It is a clear consent gate, not a permanent reminder.

Live tracking confirmation

The main screen confirms ACTIVE status, battery, last report, speed and today's kilometers. No doubt whether it is working.

Automatic power saving

When the battery runs low, TrackPhone extends the reporting interval to save charge, without stopping transmission.

The map lives on your platform

TrackPhone only transmits; the map, history and reports stay on your GPS platform, alongside the rest of your fleet.
